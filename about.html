About Novira

We exist to make the physical world trustworthy.

Novira began with a simple frustration: the world is full of data about what happens to physical things — and almost none of it can be trusted when it matters most.

Why Novira exists

Every year, enormous value is lost not because something went wrong, but because no one could prove what went wrong. A shipment arrives damaged and the argument begins. A claim is filed and stalls for want of evidence. A loss is absorbed by whoever can least afford to fight it.

The technology to observe the physical world is everywhere — sensors, trackers, dashboards. But observation isn't proof. A reading on a screen settles nothing when money is on the line, because anyone can question where it came from and whether it was changed.

We built Novira to close that gap — to turn what happens in the physical world into something the whole world can believe.

Not another tracker. Not another dashboard. A platform that takes a raw signal, verifies it, seals it as evidence, shares it with everyone who needs it, acts on it automatically, and learns from what truly happened. A chain of trust, from the moment of capture to the moment of resolution.

What we believe

The convictions behind the platform.

Truth is infrastructure.
When the facts of what happened are settled and trusted, everything downstream — claims, protection, decisions — gets faster, fairer, and cheaper. Trust isn't a feature; it's the foundation.
Be honest about the line.
We automate what can be automated and are clear about what can't. We don't overclaim, we don't dress prediction up as certainty, and we say plainly where the human stays in the loop.
The artifact is the product.
Customers don't buy sensors. They receive records they can hand to anyone — an insurer, a partner, a court — each answering one decisive question about what happened.
