Asset protection

An asset leaves its route. Act before it's gone.

A high-value asset deviates from its approved path. Here's how Novira detects it, verifies it's real, and triggers a response in the moment — instead of discovering the loss after the fact.

The problem

By the time you notice it's gone, it's gone.

High-value assets move through places you can't watch. When one is diverted, taken off-route, or moved without authorization, the window to act is short — and most systems only surface the problem after the asset, and the chance to recover it, has already disappeared. Detection without verification is just another alert to ignore.

What's at risk
Stolen or diverted assets
Slow, blind response windows
Unrecoverable losses

How Novira handles it

Six steps, from deviation to response.

The steps that matter most for this scenario are highlighted.

1
Track · observe
The asset's movement is captured continuously.
Location and motion are recorded against its approved route and zones — so a deviation registers the instant it happens.
2
NovaCheck · verify
The deviation is confirmed, not a false alarm.
NovaCheck verifies the reading is genuine and tied to the right asset — so a real diversion is distinguished from noise, and the response is warranted.
3
NovaProof · trust
The event becomes a sealed record.
A tamper-evident record of where the asset went and when — the proof you need for recovery, a claim, or law enforcement.
4
NovaLink · share
The right parties are looking at the same truth.
Operations, security, and any partner reference one verified record — so the response is coordinated, not contradictory.
5
NovaProtect · act
A verified deviation triggers a response.
The moment the unauthorized movement is confirmed, the protection workflow initiates — alert, lock, or escalation. Benefit delivery is handled with you.
6
DOST · learn
At-risk assets are flagged in advance.
DOST reads patterns across your fleet to surface the assets most likely to be at risk — so you act before a loss, not after.
The outcome
Result
An unauthorized movement is detected, verified, and acted on in the moment — with a sealed record of exactly what happened. The response begins while there's still a chance to recover, instead of after the asset is already lost.
