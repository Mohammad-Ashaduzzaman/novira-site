The problem you know too well

You can't settle fairly on evidence that doesn't exist.

Claims arrive without proof

A loss is reported, but what actually happened is murky — reconstructed from statements, estimates, and incomplete data. Every ambiguous claim is slow, costly, and a fraud risk.

And ambiguity is expensive

Without trusted facts, you over-reserve, over-investigate, and sometimes overpay. The cost of not knowing the truth is baked into every premium and every loss ratio.

How Novira helps

The same six steps — feeding your book.

1
Track · observe
The insured asset reports its own condition.
Location and condition captured continuously on the covered asset or shipment — an objective record, independent of any party's account.
2
NovaCheck · verify
Each event is verified at the source.
NovaCheck confirms every reading is genuine, unaltered, and tied to the right asset — closing the door on the ambiguity that enables fraud.
3
NovaProof · trust
The loss event becomes underwritable fact.
A sealed, tamper-evident record of exactly what happened. Evidence you can adjudicate against — and that the insured trusts too.
4
NovaLink · share
Insurer and insured see the same truth.
No dueling reports. Both sides reference one verified record, so settlements are faster and disputes rarer.
5
NovaProtect · act
Covered events can trigger response automatically.
When a verified event meets the terms, the protection workflow initiates in the moment — parametric-style speed. Benefit delivery is handled with you.
6
DOST · learn
Risk is priced on verified history.
DOST learns from verified outcomes across the book — sharpening risk models on what truly happened, not on noisy or self-reported data.
