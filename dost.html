The problem

Most insight arrives as hindsight — true, certain, and far too late to use.

You learn the pattern after the loss

The report explains what went wrong, after it's gone wrong. You see the cause clearly — once the shipment is already spoiled, the line already down, the delay already missed. Understanding without time to act is just a record of regret.

And the warning that mattered never came

Somewhere in the signals, the trouble was building. The data was there. But no one was reading it forward — turning what's happening now into what's about to happen, while there was still a window to respond.

Why this is hard

Prediction without trusted ground truth is just guessing.

Anyone can build a model that outputs a forecast. The hard part is being right — and knowing whether you were. A prediction is only as good as the truth it learns from. Train on noisy, unverified data and you get confident nonsense. DOST is different because Novira already knows what really happened. Every prediction is checked against a verified outcome — so the intelligence compounds on truth, not on noise.
