Frequently asked

The questions teams ask first.

Honest answers — including where the lines are.

What does Novira actually do?
Novira turns what happens to your physical assets into verified, trustworthy records — and acts on them. It observes an asset, verifies what it sees is real and unaltered, seals that as evidence, shares it with everyone who needs it, triggers protection workflows automatically, and learns to predict risk. The short version: it makes the physical world trustworthy, then acts on that trust.
Is Novira just another tracker?
No. Trackers tell you where something is. Novira tells you what happened to it — and proves it. A tracker produces readings you still have to trust; Novira verifies every reading and seals it as evidence you can hand to a partner, an insurer, or a court. The device is the entry point; the platform — and the verified records it produces — is the product.
Is Novira an insurance company?
No. Novira is not an insurer, underwriter, or risk carrier. NovaProtect configures and activates protection workflows when a verified event meets pre-set conditions — but the actual delivery of any benefit is handled with you and your providers. We're deliberately honest about that line: Novira automates the trigger, not the payout.
How is a NovaProof different from sensor data?
Raw sensor data is an opinion with a timestamp — anyone can question where it came from or whether it was changed. A NovaProof is a sealed, tamper-evident record that has passed verification: confirmed genuine, unaltered, and tied to the right asset at the right moment. It's the difference between "a sensor said 41°F" and "it is established that this shipment reached 41°F."
Does DOST predict the future?
DOST forecasts what's likely — never what's certain. It learns from verified outcomes (not raw noise) to flag elevated risk early enough to act on. We're careful never to dress a likelihood up as a guarantee; what makes DOST trustworthy is that it's grounded in events Novira actually verified.
What does it cost?
Three parts: the device (one-time hardware, with public pricing), a platform subscription (recurring, per device), and protection (quote-based, sized to your deployment). Small orders can be configured and purchased directly; fleets and enterprise deployments are quoted. The Pricing page walks through the full model, and Build Your Solution gives you a tailored configuration.
Which device is right for me?
It depends on what you're protecting and which conditions matter — temperature, shock, location, humidity, and so on. NovaFive is the flagship multi-sensor device; other devices suit specific needs like GPS-first tracking or specialized environments. The fastest way to find the right fit is Compare Devices, or let Build Your Solution recommend one based on your use case.
How does Novira fit my industry?
The same six-step chain — observe, verify, trust, share, act, learn — maps to the problems each industry actually faces, from cold chain to logistics to insurance to manufacturing to asset protection. The Solutions pages show the chain translated into your world, against the losses and disputes specific to it.
