The problem you know too well

A breach you can't undo. A loss you can't prove.

The product is fragile

Vaccines, biologics, specialty pharma, fresh goods — they live inside a narrow temperature band. Minutes outside it can render an entire shipment unusable, and you may not know until it's too late to save.

The dispute is worse

When a shipment arrives compromised, everyone points elsewhere. Was it the shipper, the carrier, the handoff? Without trusted evidence, the claim drags, the write-off lands somewhere, and the relationship frays.

How Novira helps

The same six steps — in your cold chain.

1
Track · observe
A multi-sensor device rides with the shipment.
Temperature, humidity, light, and location captured continuously — from packout to delivery, tied to that specific load.
2
NovaCheck · verify
A breach is confirmed, not just flagged.
When the temperature leaves the safe band, NovaCheck verifies the reading is genuine, unaltered, and from the right shipment — so it can't be disputed later.
3
NovaProof · trust
The excursion becomes sealed evidence.
A tamper-evident record: this shipment reached this temperature, here, for this long. The document you hand to a partner, an auditor, or an insurer.
4
NovaLink · share
Every party sees the same truth.
Shipper, carrier, receiver, and insurer reference one verified record — not three conflicting copies. The blame game ends before it starts.
5
NovaProtect · act
Protection activates on the verified breach.
The moment the excursion is confirmed, the protection workflow initiates — instantly, not weeks later. Benefit delivery is handled with you.
6
DOST · learn
The next breach is predicted before it happens.
DOST reads the patterns across your lanes and flags the shipments likely to breach — early enough to reroute, re-ice, or intervene while there's still time.
