The problem

A number on a screen isn't proof. It's just a number — until someone proves it.

Any reading can be doubted

Was the sensor working? Did the value get changed on its way to the dashboard? Is this even the right shipment? Every raw reading carries these questions, and as long as they're open, the reading proves nothing when it matters.

Doubt is where value leaks

An unverified reading can't settle a dispute, can't trigger protection, can't be trusted by an insurer. It's data, not evidence — and the gap between the two is exactly where claims stall and arguments begin.

Why this is hard

Trust isn't a setting you switch on. It has to be earned, every time.

To turn a reading into something defensible, you have to answer every doubt at once: that the device was genuine and functioning, that the value wasn't altered in transit, that it's tied to the right asset at the right moment, and that nothing was tampered with along the way. Miss one and the whole reading is contestable. NovaCheck runs every one of those checks, on every reading — automatically.
