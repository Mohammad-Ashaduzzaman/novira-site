The problem you know too well

When something happens to an asset, can you prove its condition?

Assets live out of sight

Equipment in the field, inventory in transit, high-value goods in storage — they spend most of their life where you can't watch them, and their condition is a question mark until something goes wrong.

And after the fact, it's too late

When an asset is damaged, lost, or disputed, reconstructing where it was and in what condition is guesswork. Without a trusted record, you absorb losses you can't contest.

How Novira helps

The same six steps — on your assets.

1
Track · observe
Every asset carries its own record.
Location, movement, and condition captured continuously — wherever the asset goes, whoever holds it, tied to that specific unit.
2
NovaCheck · verify
An event is confirmed, not assumed.
When an asset is moved, dropped, or exposed, NovaCheck verifies the reading is genuine and tied to the right asset — so it can't be disputed.
3
NovaProof · trust
Condition becomes a sealed record.
A tamper-evident record of where an asset was and in what state — the proof you produce when condition is questioned or a claim is made.
4
NovaLink · share
Custody is clear at every handoff.
As an asset changes hands, each party references the same verified record — so responsibility is never ambiguous and disputes don't fester.
5
NovaProtect · act
Loss or damage triggers a response.
When a verified event occurs — theft, damage, unauthorized movement — the protection workflow initiates in the moment. Benefit delivery is handled with you.
6
DOST · learn
Risk to your assets is anticipated.
DOST reads patterns across your fleet of assets and flags the ones most likely to be at risk — so you act before a loss, not after.
