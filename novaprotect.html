The problem

By the time protection arrives, the moment that mattered is long gone.

Protection is slow by default

Something goes wrong. Then a claim is filed. Then it waits — for review, for evidence, for someone to decide it's real. The protection you paid for shows up weeks after the event it was meant to cover, if it shows up at all.

The delay is the damage

A slow response can't reroute a shipment, can't salvage a load, can't catch the next failure before it happens. By the time the system reacts, the only thing left to do is count the loss.

Why this is hard

You can't act automatically on something you can't trust.

Automatic protection sounds simple until you ask the hard question: how do you act, instantly and without a human, on an event you're not certain actually happened? Act on a false alarm and you've paid for nothing. That's why protection has always waited for human review — the trust wasn't there to move faster. NovaProof changes that. Once an event is verified, it's safe to act on the moment it occurs.
