Platform overview

The Novira platform is a single connected chain of six steps. Each step does one job, and the value comes from the whole sequence working together.

Track — observe

Capture is where trust begins. A Novira device records what happens to an asset — location, temperature, shock, and other conditions — continuously and bound to that specific asset. Track isn't commodity GPS; it's the first witness, capturing the signal in a form built to be verified later.

NovaCheck — verify

A reading on its own is just a claim. NovaCheck confirms each significant reading is genuine, unaltered, and tied to the right asset — closing the doubts a counterparty might raise. Anyone can collect readings; the hard part is making them true.

NovaProof — trust

A verified event is sealed into a NovaProof: a tamper-evident record of exactly what happened, when, and where. This is the artifact at the heart of the platform — the document you can hand to anyone, knowing it will hold. Raw data is an opinion with a timestamp; a NovaProof is evidence.

NovaLink — share

A NovaProof is shared by reference, not by copy. Every party — shipper, carrier, insurer — sees the same verified record, so there's one source of truth instead of competing versions. Sharing the truth, not just sending a file.

NovaProtect — act

When a verified event meets pre-set conditions, NovaProtect initiates a protection workflow in the moment. Novira automates the trigger; benefit delivery is handled with you and your providers.

An important boundary: Novira is not an insurer, underwriter, or risk carrier. NovaProtect activates and coordinates protection workflows on a verified event — it does not itself pay claims or deliver benefits.

DOST — learn

DOST studies verified outcomes across your operation to forecast what's likely — never what's certain — flagging elevated risk early enough to act on. Because it learns from verified events rather than noisy data, its predictions rest on a trustworthy foundation.

Why the chain matters

Each step depends on the one before it. There's no trustworthy record without a passed verification, no useful sharing without a sealed record, no defensible action without verified grounds. The chain — not any single link — is what makes a Novira record something the whole world can act on.
